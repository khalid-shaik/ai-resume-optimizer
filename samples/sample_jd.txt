Junior Data Analyst

We are looking for a Data Analyst to join our analytics team. You will collect, clean and analyse data, build dashboards and reports in Tableau or Power BI, and present insights to stakeholders.

Requirements:
- Strong SQL and Python skills, including data cleaning and data visualization
- Experience with Excel, statistics and A/B testing
- Familiarity with ETL pipelines and cloud platforms such as AWS or BigQuery
- Good communication and storytelling skills
- Knowledge of Git and Agile ways of working

Responsibilities include building KPI dashboards, automating reporting, and working with stakeholders to define metrics.
